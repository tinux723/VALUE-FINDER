How the numbers work

Fair chance. Pinnacle is the sharpest bookmaker in the world: its odds are the market's best estimate of the true chance. The tool removes Pinnacle's margin to get a fair probability for each outcome. If Pinnacle isn't offering the match, it averages all bookmakers instead, which is less reliable.

Edge. The tool finds the best odds any bookmaker is offering and checks whether they pay more than the fair chance deserves. Edge = fair chance × odds − 1. An edge of +4% means that over many such bets you'd expect to gain about 4% of your stakes.

Picks. For each game the tool lists every common bet: result, double chance, both teams to score and over/under 1.5, 2.5 and 3.5 goals. Result and the main goals line come straight from the sharp market. The rest come from a goals model: it works out how many goals each team is expected to score from those same prices, then calculates every other market from that. A Safe pick is the likeliest bet with a 65–84% chance (odds about 1.20–1.55). A Value pick is a likely bet (40%+) where a bookmaker pays at least 2% more than fair. The Goals view is the strongest BTTS or over/under lean.
