How the numbers work

Fair chance. Pinnacle is the sharpest bookmaker in the world: its odds are the market's best estimate of the true chance. The tool removes Pinnacle's margin to get a fair probability for each outcome. If Pinnacle isn't offering the match, it averages all bookmakers instead, which is less reliable.

Edge. The tool finds the best odds any bookmaker is offering and checks whether they pay more than the fair chance deserves. Edge = fair chance × odds − 1. An edge of +4% means that over many such bets you'd expect to gain about 4% of your stakes.

Your local bookmaker. Bet9ja, SportyBet and other Nigerian bookmakers aren't in the data feed. Type their odds into the box on any market to see whether their price has an edge.

Match stats. For football, API-Football adds form, goal averages, head-to-head numbers and its own prediction model as a second opinion. Weather comes from Open-Meteo (free, no key needed).
